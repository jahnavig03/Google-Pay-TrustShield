## Method

- Payment scenarios carry their own payer/payee features (`payment_scenarios.jsonl`), so results don't depend on `data/mock/features.json`.
- Messages are wrapped in a usual-amount payment to a long-standing payee; the predicted pattern is the response `risk_type` (anything that is not a scam pattern counts as `none`).
- Baseline for FP reduction = context-free rules: amount ≥ 5× median, payee < 24 h old, or new device.
- Latency = wall time of `evaluate_payment_async` (ADK workflow incl. Runner set-up), after one warm-up call.
- Caveat: the mock-mode keyword rules (`config/scam_rules.yaml`) were tuned while building this set, so message metrics are optimistic. Re-run with `--mode gcp` and add a held-out set (e.g. from Ronak's generator) before quoting them.
